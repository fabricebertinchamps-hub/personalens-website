How to export a WhatsApp chat as a text file

Open the chat in WhatsApp, open the chat's menu (tap the name at the top on iPhone, or the three dots then More on Android), tap Export chat, and choose Without media. WhatsApp produces a plain-text file of the conversation (zipped on iPhone) that you can save, email to yourself, or open in any analysis tool. The steps below cover both phones, what the file contains, the limits, and the Telegram equivalent.

Export a WhatsApp chat on iPhone

  1. Open WhatsApp and go into the conversation you want (a one-to-one chat or a group).
  2. Tap the contact or group name at the top of the screen.
  3. Scroll to the bottom of the info page and tap Export Chat.
  4. Choose Without Media (more on this below).
  5. In the share sheet, choose Save to Files, or send it to yourself by email or AirDrop.

On iPhone the export arrives as a .zip file. Inside is a text file called _chat.txt. Most tools that accept WhatsApp exports, including Persona Lens, read the .zip directly, so you do not need to unzip it.

Export a WhatsApp chat on Android

  1. Open the conversation in WhatsApp.
  2. Tap the three dots in the top right corner.
  3. Tap More, then Export chat.
  4. Choose Without media.
  5. Pick where to send it: Google Drive, Gmail, or another app. Saving to Drive or your Files app keeps it off email servers.

On Android you usually get a text file named something like WhatsApp Chat with Sam.txt. Depending on your phone and WhatsApp version it may also come zipped.

Can I export from WhatsApp Web or the desktop app?

No. Export chat is a phone feature. If you want the file on your computer, export on the phone and then email it to yourself, or save it to a cloud drive and open it there.

"Without media" or "Include media"?

For almost every purpose, choose Without media. The text of the conversation is identical either way. "Include media" adds photos, videos, voice notes and documents as separate attachments, which makes the file far larger and slower to share, and most analysis tools ignore media anyway. Where a photo or voice note was sent, the text file shows a placeholder line such as <Media omitted> or "image omitted", so you can still see that something was shared and when.

There is also a practical limit. WhatsApp's own help centre has long stated that an export can contain up to about 40,000 of the most recent messages without media, but only around 10,000 with media, because of attachment size constraints. If your chat is very long, exporting without media gets you more history. For years-long chats, the oldest messages may simply not be included.

What the exported file looks like

Each message is one line (or several, if the message itself had line breaks), starting with a timestamp and the sender's name as it appears in your contacts:

[14/03/2026, 21:04:12] Sam: are we still on for saturday?
[14/03/2026, 21:31:40] You: yes! 7pm?
[14/03/2026, 22:58:03] Sam: 👍

A few details worth knowing:

  • Date format follows your phone's region settings. A chat exported in the UK shows day/month; one exported in the US shows month/day. Good tools detect this automatically, but it matters if you are reading the file yourself.
  • iPhone and Android format lines differently. iPhone wraps the timestamp in square brackets; Android usually writes 14/03/2026, 21:04 - Sam: .... Both are fine.
  • Deleted messages appear as "This message was deleted", and edited messages may be marked as edited. The original text of deleted messages is not recoverable from the export.
  • System lines are included: people joining or leaving a group, changes to the group name, security-code notices. Analysis tools should filter these out.
  • Names are whatever your phone calls them. If someone is saved as "Mum" in your contacts, that is how they appear. If they are not in your contacts, you will see their number.

That timestamp column is more useful than it looks. Who starts conversations, how fast each person replies, and whether that tempo has changed over months are all visible in it. Our guide to what reply time actually means explains which of those signals are meaningful and which are noise.

How to export a Telegram chat

Telegram works differently: the phone apps cannot export chat history. You need Telegram Desktop on a computer.

  1. Install and log in to Telegram Desktop (Windows, macOS or Linux).
  2. Open the conversation.
  3. Click the three dots at the top right and choose Export chat history.
  4. Untick photos, videos and other media.
  5. Under format, choose Machine-readable JSON if you plan to use an analysis tool, or HTML if you just want to read it in a browser. You can also limit the date range.
  6. Click Export. You will get a folder containing result.json.

If you only have your phone, you have two options. You can select and copy messages in the Telegram app and paste them into a tool that accepts pasted text. Or, in Persona Lens, you can connect Telegram directly from the browser: you sign in with the code Telegram sends you, pick a chat, and the app reads its recent messages. That session is stored on your own device.

iMessage, Signal, Instagram and others

Most other messengers have no clean export for a single chat. The reliable route is copy and paste: select the stretch of conversation you care about, copy it, and paste it into the tool. For analysis, make sure each line starts with who said it, like Sam: are we still on?. Without speaker names, no tool can tell who wrote what.

How much of the chat should you export?

Usually, all of it, and let the tool decide what to use. More messages give a steadier reading, and a single tense evening can look very different from the three months around it. As a rough guide, around 100 messages supports only a broad reading, 300 or more gives a sharper one, and several hundred to a few thousand is where patterns become reliable. Our guide to how many messages you need to analyse someone's personality explains why.

The exception is a very specific question. If you want to understand one argument, export the whole chat but tell the tool, or yourself, which dates to focus on.

Handle the file with care

WhatsApp messages are end-to-end encrypted in transit and on WhatsApp's servers. The export is not. Once you tap Export, you have an ordinary text file that anyone with access to your email, cloud drive or downloads folder can read. A few habits help:

  • Save to your phone's files or a private cloud folder rather than emailing it, where you can.
  • Delete the export from Downloads and your sent email once you are done with it.
  • Before uploading to any website, check what that site does with it. Our guide is it safe to upload your WhatsApp chat to an AI website? gives a checklist.
  • Remember that the chat contains someone else's words too. Exporting your own conversation is normal; sharing it widely is a different matter.

Troubleshooting

"Export chat" is missing

Update WhatsApp to the latest version and look again in the chat's info page (iPhone) or the three-dot menu under More (Android). Some business and managed accounts restrict exports. If it is still missing, copy and paste is the fallback.

The file is huge or will not send

You probably included media. Export again and choose Without media.

The tool says it cannot read the file

Check that you uploaded the text file or the .zip itself, not a screenshot or a PDF. If you opened and re-saved the .txt in a word processor, the format may have changed; export again and upload the original.

Group chats show phone numbers instead of names

That is how WhatsApp writes members who are not in your contacts. Group analysis still works; you may just need to tell the tool which number is which person. For more on what a group thread reveals, see group chat dynamics decoded.

Frequently asked questions

How do I export a WhatsApp chat as a text file?

Open the chat, tap the contact or group name (iPhone) or the three dots then More (Android), tap Export chat and choose Without media. WhatsApp creates a text file of the conversation, zipped on iPhone, which you can save to Files, Google Drive or email to yourself.

Is there a limit to how many WhatsApp messages you can export?

Yes. WhatsApp's help centre has stated that an export includes up to about 40,000 recent messages without media, or about 10,000 with media. For very long chats, export without media to keep as much history as possible.

Can I export a WhatsApp chat from WhatsApp Web or the desktop app?

No. Exporting is only available in the phone app. Export on your phone, then send the file to your computer by email or a cloud drive.

How do I export a Telegram chat on my phone?

The Telegram phone apps cannot export chat history; you need Telegram Desktop, using the three-dot menu and Export chat history. On a phone you can copy and paste messages instead, or connect Telegram directly inside a tool that supports it, such as Persona Lens.

Is an exported WhatsApp chat still encrypted?

No. End-to-end encryption protects messages inside WhatsApp. The exported file is plain text, so store it privately, delete it when you are finished, and only upload it to services whose data handling you have checked.
